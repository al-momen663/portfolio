Research

Active Research · Health Informatics Lab

Beating MambaPhase: knowledge distillation for liquid-liquid phase separation protein classification.

Building knowledge distillation (KD) pipelines using Mamba-based architectures — ParallelSSM, GatedMambaBlock, SwiGLU FFN, cross-attention fusion — to surpass the benchmarks established by the MambaPhase paper published in Briefings in Bioinformatics (2025).

The pipeline integrates contrastive loss, CutMix augmentation, and architectural improvements over the teacher-student framework. Iterated across v1, v2, and v3 of the Mamba KD pipeline on Kaggle with GPU T4×2 acceleration.
